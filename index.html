Airfreight Sort — Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 17, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Airfreight Sort (the "Game"), published by Mezhenon Production (the "Developer", "we", "us"), handles information when you play the Game on Android.

Adults only (18+).

The Game is intended exclusively for users aged 18 or older. It is not directed to children or minors. The Game includes optional rewarded advertisements and may offer purchases using real money. Players are never required to watch an advertisement in order to proceed to the next level.

If you are under 18, please do not use the Game or make purchases. If we learn that personal data from a minor has been collected in connection with the Game, we will take appropriate steps to address it, including requesting deletion where applicable.

1. Information handled by the Game

The Game is designed to work without creating an account or providing your name. The Game may save gameplay progress, language preferences, sound settings and virtual currency balances locally on your device. These local records are used to keep your progress and preferences between sessions.

Third-party services integrated into the Game may automatically process information such as your device's advertising identifier (when available and permitted), IP address, approximate location inferred from the IP address, device and app information, ad interactions, purchase information, and technical diagnostics. The Developer does not need your precise GPS location to operate the Game and does not intentionally request precise location permission.

2. Optional rewarded advertising

The Game uses Google AdMob to offer optional rewarded ads. You may choose to watch an ad in exchange for an in-game reward, such as coins or extra moves. There are no mandatory ads between levels as part of the intended advertising model. Declining an optional ad does not prevent normal gameplay.

Google and its advertising partners may process device identifiers, IP address, ad performance data and related information to deliver, measure, secure and, where permitted, personalize advertising. Depending on your region and choices, ads may be personalized or non-personalized.

For details, see Google Privacy Policy and Google advertising information .

3. Advertising consent and privacy choices

Where legally required, including in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, we use a consent-management flow (such as Google's User Messaging Platform, or UMP) to present relevant advertising privacy choices.

Your choices may affect whether personalized advertisements are shown and how advertising-related data is processed.

Where a privacy-options control is required, the Game should provide a way to revisit and change consent choices. You may also use your Android device's privacy settings to manage or reset its advertising ID, where supported.

Withdrawing consent does not necessarily prevent all processing that is allowed or required under other lawful grounds.

4. In-app purchases

The Game may offer optional purchases, including virtual coins, cosmetic items or premium bundles, through Google Play Billing. Payments are processed by Google Play. We do not receive or store your full payment card number or payment credentials.

Google Play may share limited purchase information with the Game, such as purchase status, product identifiers and transaction tokens, to validate transactions, deliver purchased content, prevent fraud and restore eligible purchases.

Please see the Google Privacy Policy for how Google handles payments and associated data.

Purchases are optional. Any pricing and purchase confirmation appear in Google Play's purchase flow. Refund requests are handled under applicable law and Google Play's policies.

5. Why information is used

  • To operate the Game and save progress and settings.
  • To display optional rewarded ads and grant rewards after eligible ad completions.
  • To provide, validate and, where applicable, restore in-app purchases.
  • To maintain security, detect fraud and troubleshoot technical problems.
  • To meet legal obligations and honor applicable privacy choices.

6. Third-party services and international processing

Relevant third-party services include Google AdMob / Google Mobile Ads SDK, Google's consent-management tools and Google Play Billing. These providers may handle data under their own privacy policies and may process information in countries other than the one in which you live, subject to applicable safeguards.

We do not sell player contact lists or directly operate a marketplace for personal information. Advertising providers may process or share certain information for advertising services as described in their policies and in consent notices.

7. Data retention, deletion and security

Local game progress and settings generally remain on your device until you clear the Game's app data or uninstall it, subject to Android backup behavior and platform settings.

Clearing local data may permanently erase progress, virtual currency records or preferences.

Google and other integrated providers determine their own retention periods for information they process. You may use Google's privacy controls and contact the Developer regarding a privacy request.

We take reasonable measures to protect the information under our control, but no digital system can guarantee absolute security.

8. Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, erase, restrict or object to certain processing of your personal information, or to withdraw consent. You may also have the right to lodge a complaint with your data-protection authority.

For information handled by Google, use Google's privacy tools or contact Google directly. For Developer-related inquiries, contact us using the address below.

We may need enough information to identify the relevant request, but will not ask you to provide unnecessary personal data.

9. Children's privacy

The Game is not designed for, marketed to, or intended for people under the age of 18. We do not knowingly solicit personal information from children.

If you believe a minor has provided personal data through the Game, please contact us so we can evaluate and address the issue.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the Game's features, service providers or legal requirements change. We will publish the revised text at the same public URL and update the effective date.

Material changes may require additional in-app notice or consent where applicable.
